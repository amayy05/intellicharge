# IntelliCharge — Human Usability Test Kit

> **Important:** No real participants have been tested yet. Results sections are intentionally blank.
> This document is a preparation kit for recruiting and observing 10 real human participants.

---

## Moderator Script

### Before the Session
1. Welcome the participant and thank them for their time.
2. Explain: "We're testing an app that helps EV drivers find charging stations near them. We want to learn how easy or difficult it is to use — not your skills, but the app's design."
3. Read the consent note (below) and obtain verbal or written consent.
4. Confirm screen sharing is set up (remote) or screen recording is running (in-person, with consent).
5. Ask: "Please think aloud as you work through each task — tell me what you're seeing, thinking, and expecting."

### During the Session
- Do NOT lead the participant to the answer.
- If they ask for help, respond: "What would you try next?" or "What do you expect to happen here?"
- Note where they hesitate, backtrack, or express confusion.
- Note exact quote if participant says something insightful (with consent to quote anonymously).

### After Tasks
- Run the debrief questions (Section 6).
- Thank participant and confirm next steps.

## Consent and Privacy Note

*Read aloud or share in writing before the session:*

> We are testing the IntelliCharge app. During this session, we will record your screen (and voice/video only with your additional consent). We will not collect your real name, email, real vehicle details, or payment information. Please use the synthetic vehicle and sample data provided. Your participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time. Recordings will be used only for design improvements and deleted within 30 days. Your feedback may be quoted anonymously in internal reports — only with your separate consent to quote.

## Task Sheet (Give to Participant)

**Participant Code:** ___________  
**Date:** _____________  
**Device/Browser:** _____________

Use the staging app at: [staging URL — to be filled when deployed]

*Please use the synthetic vehicle details below (do NOT enter your real car or address):*
- **Vehicle:** Sample EV — 40 kWh battery, CCS2 connector
- **Location:** Use the default Palghar location shown in the app

---

### Task 1 — First Impression
Open the app and, without clicking anything, tell me what you think it does.  
*(Moderator: Wait for at least 30 seconds before allowing scrolling.)*

### Task 2 — Find a Charging Station
Find a charging station that could work for the sample vehicle above.

### Task 3 — Compare Two Stations
Look at any two stations and tell me which one you would choose, and why.

### Task 4 — Get a Recommendation
Enter the sample vehicle details and ask the app for a recommendation.

### Task 5 — Join a Queue *(only if queue feature is available)*
Try to join the queue at the recommended station.

### Task 6 — Check Your Status *(only if applicable)*
Find where your queue or charging session status is shown. Explain what it means to you.

### Task 7 — Handle a Problem
*(Moderator: ask participant to enter an obviously wrong SoC, e.g., current=90, target=20)*  
Try to join the queue with those numbers and tell me what happens.

### Task 8 — Get Help
Where would you go to get help or report a problem with the app?

## Debrief Questions

1. What was easiest?
2. What was confusing or unclear?
3. Did any information look fake, untrustworthy, or made-up?
4. What would stop you from using this app?
5. What one change would most improve it?
6. Overall ease rating: 1 (very hard) — 5 (very easy): ___
7. Would you use it again? Yes / No / Unsure — Why?
